UCSB Lambda Chi Alpha Reborn

Welcome Brothers & Friends!

Building Our Future · Preserving Our Brotherhood

Six months ago, we shared a vision to restore Lambda Chi Alpha to the UCSB campus and transform our chapter house for future generations. Today, we're excited to report that real progress is underway. For the first time in many years, National leadership, alumni leadership, and the House Corporation are moving together with a common vision for the future of Zeta Eta.

Under Construction

House Renovation Underway

Construction has begun on the modernization of our chapter house, with anticipated completion in July 2027.

The redesigned facility will feature 23 modern residential rooms with private bathrooms, creating an outstanding home for future generations of Lambda Chi brothers.

We'll begin sharing construction photographs and milestone updates as work progresses so everyone can follow the transformation. Many thanks to Ron Wolfe and the House Corporation for managing this impressive project.

Momentum Is Building

Clearing a Path to Recolonization

Several alumni recently attended the Lambda Chi Alpha General Assembly in Kansas City and met with the CEO, COO, and other National Lambda Chi officers.

They expressed strong support for returning the Zeta Eta Chapter to active status and are committed to working alongside our alumni as we move forward. Together, we are developing a strategic plan to re-establish Lambda Chi Alpha at UCSB. The emphasis is on recruiting outstanding young men, building a strong brotherhood first, and creating a chapter positioned for long-term success.

Lambda Chi's mission is to inspire and equip men to lead an ethical life of growth, service and leadership. This means building young men of character, competence, confidence and purpose.

We're also expanding relationships with UCSB's new administration, alumni leadership, and Lambda Chi Alpha National to build support for recolonization. The National Fraternity leadership team reminded us that the Zeta Eta experience at UCSB is not unique — many chapters have successfully navigated similar challenges over the past decade. The proven approach of the National Fraternity has helped establish Lambda Chi Alpha as the fastest-growing fraternity in the nation over the past five years.

Alumni Spotlight

Congratulations to Brother Ron Wolfe

One of the highlights of this year's General Assembly.

Our own Ron Wolfe received Lambda Chi Alpha's prestigious Order of Merit — one of the Fraternity's highest honors, recognizing extraordinary service and leadership.

Ron has provided more than 50 years of dedicated service to Lambda Chi Alpha, including over three decades as president of the Zeta Eta Zeta Housing Corporation. His decades of leadership and professional expertise have ensured the preservation, management, and long-term viability of the chapter property, even following the chapter's closure in 2004, and made today's renovation possible.

Looking Ahead

Opportunities to Reconnect

UCSB is expected to host its annual Alumni Weekend in October, and discussions are underway about holding a concurrent Lambda Chi Alpha alumni gathering in Santa Barbara. We hope many brothers will be able to attend, reconnect, and celebrate the exciting progress taking place.

Additional project updates, construction photos, and alumni events will be shared throughout the coming months.

How You Can Help

The future is wide open.

The success of these efforts depends on the strength of our alumni network. Many of us met lifelong friends and molded our character within the walls of the Lambda Chi house. Our goal is simple — to ensure future generations of UCSB students have the same opportunity. Whether you can contribute your time, professional expertise, campus connections, or financial support, every brother has an opportunity to help write the next chapter of Zeta Eta's history.
